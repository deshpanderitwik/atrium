## process/

Single-use microsites that articulate design decisions for Atrium.
Each one is a self-contained static page. They are scratch artifacts —
think of them as legible thinking, not documentation. Spin one up to
externalize the shape of a decision, share it, then move on.

- `00-proposal/` — the founding brief. What Atrium is, what it isn't, the twelve houses, the two screens.
